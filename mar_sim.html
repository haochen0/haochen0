Surface conditions remain ostensibly similar to the baseline, but internal dynamics begin to drift. The rhythmic weathering of silicate rocks—Earth's natural CO₂ thermostat—loses its multi-million-year calibration. Feedback loops begin to lose their "harmonic resonance."

📍 Phase 2: Accelerated Bifurcation (~2.0—1.0 Ma)

Chronic Anoxic Events become widespread, leading to a catastrophic contraction of deep-sea biodiversity.

The Stagnation Trap: Early Homo populations show a marked deceleration in encephalization. Without the pulse of extreme glacial cycles forcing technological innovation, hominids remain in "ecologically stable" zones, their evolutionary trajectory hitting a premature plateau.

The Permanent Absence of Civilization

The Neolithic Revolution required four converging conditions: the abrupt end of the Last Glacial Period, megafaunal extinction, demographic pressure, and cognitive readiness. In the Mars-less world, **zero** of these conditions are met.

Synthesis and Philosophical Implications

Mars possesses only one-tenth the mass of Earth, yet from a distance of 78 million kilometers, it plucks two invisible gravitational strings that have determined the destiny of terrestrial life.

The 100,000-year string ignited the spark of human civilization through environmental adversity. The 2.4-million-year string maintains the long-term metabolic respiration of Earth as a "living planet." Sever either string, and civilization never emerges. Sever both, and Earth becomes a silent, pale blue rock—possessing oceans and an atmosphere, but devoid of the complexity of history.
