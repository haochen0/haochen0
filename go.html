How to Play: Assimilation Go

This is a variant of Go (Weiqi) where captured stones are converted rather than removed.

  • Objective: Have more stones of your color on the board than your opponent.
  • Capture (Assimilation): If a group of stones loses all its "liberties" (empty adjacent intersections), they are not removed. Instead, they turn into the opponent's color.
  • Ko Rule: You cannot make a move that repeats the exact previous board state.
  • Suicide: You cannot place a stone that would immediately have no liberties, unless it captures enemy stones.
